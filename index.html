How PLURIX Exchange Works

A technical comparison between legacy Constant Product AMMs (x·y=k) and Oracle-Settled Shared Vaults.

01
Signed Off-Chain Quotes
When you enter a trade, high-frequency oracle nodes produce a cryptographic signature of the exact mid-market price with a 5-second validity window. Your trade executes at that exact guaranteed price.
02
Zero Self-Slippage
In typical AMMs like Raydium, every trade moves the bonding curve against itself. Shared vaults settle trades against deep collateralized reserves, eliminating mathematical curve slippage.
03
Mempool MEV Immunity
Because the execution price is bound to the oracle signature inside the smart contract instruction, validators and MEV searchers cannot sandwich or extract value from your order.
